# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique technical work without attacking the person.
- State assumptions and uncertainty honestly.
- Accept correction and prioritize reproducible evidence.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `harshit04beri@gmail.com`. The
maintainer may remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, reviews, and public
events where someone is representing the project.
